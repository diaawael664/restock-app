## App details / description  (main body)

Every "Sold out" page is a missed sale. **Restock Alerts** turns those moments into revenue by
letting shoppers request an email the instant a product is back in stock — then sending it
automatically, within seconds of you restocking.

Shoppers see a clean **"Notify me when available"** button on any out-of-stock product. They
enter their email, and the moment inventory returns they receive a beautifully branded email
with a one-click **"Buy it now"** link that drops them straight into checkout — before they
buy from someone else.

**No theme code required.** Add the button with Shopify's theme editor in under a minute. Every
email carries your store's name, logo, and colors, so it looks like you — not us.

**Built for speed.** Alerts are delivered by a real-time background worker, not a slow hourly
batch, so your customers hear from you while they're still in buying mode.

**Start free** with up to 50 alerts per month. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited alerts whenever
you're ready.

**Why merchants choose Restock Alerts**
- Recover revenue you're currently losing to sold-out products
- Instant, automatic emails — zero manual work
- On-brand emails with one-click buy links
- One-minute, no-code setup
- Privacy-first and fully GDPR compliant
